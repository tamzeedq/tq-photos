About

I’m Tamzeed, a hobbyist photographer based in Vancouver. Most weekends I’m on a trail somewhere in the Coast Mountains. When I travel, I walk cities until I’ve lost the map.

I try to photograph places the way they felt to stand in. That usually means waiting for weather, and keeping the edit light.
